## Scientific source and exactness audit
- **Selberg:** five classical axioms: absolute Dirichlet series, meromorphic continuation with possible pole at one, gamma-factor functional equation, Ramanujan coefficient estimate for every epsilon, Euler-product/prime-power log coefficient bound. Sources: [Farmer, Mathematika (2021)](https://londmathsoc.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1112/mtk.12093), [scholarly exposition](https://www.math.ubc.ca/~gerg/teaching/613-Winter2011/SelbergClass.pdf). Does NOT assert classification or all automorphic L-functions in S.
- **Grand RH:** convention is for standard automorphic L-functions with suitably normalized functional-equation central lines; family and normalization matter. GL(1)/GL(2)/GL(n) are **contextual example doorways**, not assertion of universal inclusion. References: [J. Arthur lectures](https://www.math.toronto.edu/arthur/pdf/automorphic-L.pdf) and [automorphic L-function research paper (2021)](https://www.mdpi.com/2227-7390/9/17/2061). No Langlands proof claimed.
- **Dirichlet GRH:** all Dirichlet L-function nontrivial zeros on real part 1/2, using NIST [DLMF §25.15](https://dlmf.nist.gov/25.15). The exact example `χ₄` assigns +1 to n≡1 mod 4, −1 to n≡3 mod4, 0 to even n. The finite series at s=2 is `∑(n≤N)χ₄(n)/n²`; the absolute tail is rigorously bounded by `∑(n>N)1/n² ≤ 1/N` (elementary integral comparison). It is **not** an L-function zero finder or a GRH certificate.
- **ERH:** explicitly means Dedekind zeta critical-line conjecture for **every** number field, not the universal meaning of initials ERH. In Gaussian ring Z[i], 2 ramifies, p≡1 mod4 splits, and p≡3 mod4 is inert ([Keith Conrad, Theorem 9.7](https://kconrad.math.uconn.edu/math5230f12/handouts/Zinotes.pdf), PDF p.32 printed). The three local Dedekind Euler factors `(1−2⁻ˢ)⁻¹`, `(1−p⁻ˢ)⁻²`, `(1−p⁻²ˢ)⁻¹` correspond to prime ideal norms ([Harvard number-field course notes](https://people.math.harvard.edu/~achenjang/assets/pdf/Fall_2020_Course_Notes.pdf)). These are exact algebra, never numerical zeros.
- All four regions visibly disclaim implication from nesting. No newly verified zero, numerical RH proof, original theorem, current frontier or preprint acceptance is asserted.
