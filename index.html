HOW TO READ THE MAP

A metaphor, not a proof.

This citadel is a spatial analogy to help explore connected problems. An outer ring is not necessarily stronger than or logically inclusive of an inner ring. In particular, ERH and GRH depend on definitions, and zero-free regions are regions in the complex plane — not literal rooms.

Click any ring or room to inspect it. Zoom with your mouse wheel or the +/− buttons, pan by dragging empty space, and use the breadcrumbs to move back. The Great Hall and Courtyard contain a second level of clickable sites.
